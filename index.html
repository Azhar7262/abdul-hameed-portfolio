01 — About Me

Turning coordination into measurable impact

I'm Abdul Hameed, a Computer Science graduate from Bacha Khan University, Charsadda, currently serving as District Coordinator on a UNDP-funded project in Swat focused on women's participation, environmental justice and peacebuilding.

Over the last few years I've worked across the development sector — from job placement with Hashoo Foundation's PSDF project, to computer literacy training in the hospitality sector, to finance and bookkeeping earlier in my career. I bridge the gap between field teams, government departments and donors: planning activities, maintaining MOVs, tracking progress and turning field data into reports that keep projects on track.

I combine strong IT skills (MS Office, networking fundamentals, data management) with finance knowledge and a passion for training people — that mix is what makes projects deliver.
